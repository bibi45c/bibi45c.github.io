This blog will collect material that is useful to revisit, share, and improve over time.

## Main areas

- **Data engineering:** data pipelines, SQL, Spark, migration work, and reporting systems.
- **AI systems:** applied machine learning, evaluation, and the engineering work around reliable AI features.
- **DevSecOps:** testing, CI/CD, automation, and secure delivery practices.
- **Software projects:** implementation notes and retrospectives from coursework and portfolio projects.

Articles can begin as concise notes and grow into complete technical write-ups when the evidence and explanation are ready.
